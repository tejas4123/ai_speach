# Payments and Cancellations

## Payment options
Customers can pay with UPI, credit or debit cards, net banking, wallets, or cash on delivery (COD). COD is available for orders up to 50,000 rupees.

## No-cost EMI
No-cost EMI is available on orders above 3,000 rupees with major bank credit cards, for 3, 6 or 9 months. The interest is given back as an instant discount, so the customer pays only the product price.

## Payment deducted but order not placed
If money was deducted but no order was created, the amount is auto-refunded to the source within 3 to 5 working days. No action is needed from the customer.

## Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled free of charge from the Orders page any time before they are shipped. After shipping, the customer can refuse delivery and get a full refund once the package returns to the warehouse.

## Invoices and GST
A GST invoice is generated for every order. Business customers can add their GSTIN at checkout to claim input tax credit.
