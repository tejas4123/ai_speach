# Warranty

## Standard warranty
All products carry the manufacturer's warranty, usually 1 year for electronics and 2 years for large appliances. The warranty card and invoice are needed for a claim. The invoice can be downloaded from the Orders page.

## How to claim warranty
Warranty claims are handled by the brand's authorised service centres. Kiran Electronics can share the nearest service centre and the brand's helpline number. For products under 7 days old, a replacement is handled by Kiran Electronics directly.

## Extended warranty
Kiran Care extended warranty adds 1 or 2 extra years and can be bought within 30 days of delivery. It covers manufacturing defects, not accidental or liquid damage.

## Accidental damage
Accidental and liquid damage are not covered by the standard warranty. Kiran Care Plus, sold only at the time of purchase, covers one accidental damage repair per year.
