# Delivery and Shipping

## Delivery times
Metro cities such as Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata and Pune get delivery in 1 to 3 days. Other cities take 3 to 6 days. Remote areas and the North-East can take up to 8 days.

## Delivery charges
Delivery is free on orders above 499 rupees. Orders below 499 rupees have a 49 rupee delivery charge. Large appliances such as TVs, washing machines and refrigerators have free delivery and free installation booking.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be changed only before the order is shipped. After shipping, the customer can ask the courier to reschedule, but not to change the address.

## Missed or failed delivery
The courier tries delivery up to 3 times. If all attempts fail, the order goes back to the warehouse and a full refund is issued automatically.

## Damaged on arrival
If the package arrives damaged or tampered, the customer should refuse the delivery or report it within 48 hours with photos. A replacement or refund is then arranged.
