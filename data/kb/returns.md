# Returns and Refunds

## Return window
Most products can be returned within 10 days of delivery. Mobile phones, laptops and tablets have a 7 day replacement-only window: they can be replaced if faulty, but not returned for a refund. Items must be in original packaging with all accessories, bills and free gifts.

## Items that cannot be returned
Earphones and earbuds that have been opened, software and gift cards, and any product with physical damage caused by the customer cannot be returned. Products marked "non-returnable" on the product page are also excluded.

## How to start a return
Returns are started from the Orders page in the app or website by choosing "Return or replace". A pickup is scheduled within 2 working days. The courier checks the product at pickup, so keep it ready with the box and accessories.

## Refund timelines
Once the returned product passes the warehouse quality check, refunds are issued within 2 working days. UPI and wallet refunds usually arrive within 24 hours. Credit and debit card refunds take 5 to 7 working days to show up, depending on the bank. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to a bank account or UPI ID the customer provides.

## Replacement
A replacement is shipped as soon as the faulty item is picked up. If the same model is out of stock, the customer is offered a full refund instead.
